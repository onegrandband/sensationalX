How to Spot AI-Generated Images

As Artificial Intelligence (AI) art becomes more lifelike, telling the difference between real photos and machine-made images is getting harder. Here is how you can detect AI-generated pictures.

1. Look at the Details

AI models often struggle with complex shapes. Look closely at these areas:

  • Hands and faces: AI frequently distorts fingers, adds extra limbs, or creates blank facial expressions.
  • Text: Letters and words in the background or on signs often look like meaningless scribbles.
  • Backgrounds: Background objects may melt into each other, blur strangely, or lose realistic depth.

2. Check the Physics of Light

Natural cameras capture real light. AI simulates it, often with mistakes:

  • Shadows: Shadows may fall in different directions or fail to match the light source.
  • Reflections: Reflections in mirrors or water may not match the actual object in the image.

3. Use Digital Detection Tools

When your eyes are not enough, use online tools to check image files:

  • Reverse Image Search: Use Google or TinEye to find where the image first appeared online.
  • Metadata Checkers: Tools like OpenAI Verify scan for hidden AI signatures.
  • AI Detectors: Upload images to platforms like DeepAI or SightEngine to get a percentage score of AI probability.

Summary

Always question images that look too perfect or stir up strong emotions. Combining visual checks with AI detection tools helps you spot fakes quickly.
