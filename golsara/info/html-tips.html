Latest Tech Tips

Tip 1: Clear Your Cache

Your web browser stores old data. If your page does not load right, clear your cache. Press Ctrl + F5 on Windows or Cmd + Shift + R on Mac to fix this.

Tip 2: Make Your Text Bold

Use the bold tag to make key words pop. Type <strong> before your text and </strong> after it.

Tip 3: Add Links

Send users to other pages using the anchor tag. Use <a href="url"> to link your Golsara content.
