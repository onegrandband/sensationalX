How to Detect AI Videos: A Guide for Golsara Readers

Artificial intelligence makes it easy to create fake videos. These videos can look and sound real. Spotting them is important to avoid scams and false information. Use these simple methods to detect AI-generated videos.

1. Check for Watermarks and Visual Glitches

AI video generators leave clues in the picture. Look for visual inconsistencies like weird hand movements, bad lighting, or scrambled text on signs and badges. Also, check the edges of the video for blurry spots. Sometimes, creators blur out original AI watermarks.

2. Watch the Movement and Physics

Real movements follow the laws of physics. AI videos often break these rules. Watch background characters closely; they may repeat the same motion like a loop. Faces are another major clue. Check if a person is blinking normally. If their lips do not match the words they are saying, the video is likely fake.

3. Listen Carefully to the Audio

Audio is often harder to fake than video. Listen for a robotic or unnatural voice tone. AI voices often do not take breaths between words. If the voice sounds too perfect or emotionally flat, be suspicious.

4. Use Digital Verification Tools

Technology can help fight fake media. You can scan a suspicious video file using online deepfake scanners like [Deepware Scanner](https://deepware.ai/). These tools scan for synthetic patterns and tell you if a video was digitally altered.

5. Analyze the Source and Context

Always question the source of a video. If a shocking video of a major news event only appears on one social media account, treat it as a fake. Use [Google Search](https://google.com) to cross-reference the event with credible news outlets.

Quick Tip: Common sense is your best defense. If a video tries to scare you, or asks for money, verify the information through a completely different channel before believing it.
