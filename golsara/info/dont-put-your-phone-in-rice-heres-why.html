Why You Should Never Put a Wet Phone in Rice

It is a myth! Rice does not absorb water fast enough to save your phone from rust and short circuits.

Why Rice is Bad

  • Dust: Rice dust can get inside your device and damage internal parts.
  • Starch: Rice starch mixes with water to form a sticky paste inside your charging port.
  • Slow: Rice absorbs water too slowly to stop the parts from breaking.

What to Do Instead

  • Turn the phone off right away.
  • Wipe the outside dry with a clean cloth.
  • Place your phone in a dry room with a fan blowing cool air into the ports.
  • Use silica gel packets instead of rice to absorb water.
