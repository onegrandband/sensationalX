What It Really Means to Be Trans

About Understanding Gender The Journey Resources

Being transgender means that a person's inner sense of being male, female, or another gender does not match the sex they were assigned at birth. It is a natural part of human diversity and has existed throughout history.

Core Concept: Gender identity is a person's deep, internal sense of being male, female, or something else. Gender expression is how a person shows their gender to the world.

Understanding Gender

Every person has a gender identity. For most people, this matches their birth sex. For transgender people, these do not align. Being trans is about self-awareness, personal truth, and living a life that matches who you truly are.

The Journey is Personal

Every trans person has a unique path. Transitioning looks different for everyone. Some people change their name, clothing, or pronouns. Others choose medical care like hormone therapy or surgery. Many people do none of these things. There is no single "right" way to be trans.
