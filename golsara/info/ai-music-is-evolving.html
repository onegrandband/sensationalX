How to Detect AI Music

Golsara (by SXTeam)

Artificial intelligence makes creating songs fast and easy. However, it often leaves clues behind. You can spot these AI songs by checking for audio errors, listening for unnatural vocal sounds, and using automated tech tools.

1. Look for Audio Errors

AI music models make odd mistakes. Listen for instruments that drift in and out of the song without a clear purpose. Check for weird background noise that sounds like static or clicking. These are called artifacts. Check if the high-end is being destroyed and see if a sound effect (like a kick or snare) changes overtime.

2. Listen to the Vocals

Human singers breathe between words and change how they sound. AI vocals often lack real breathing noises. Listen to the lyrics. AI songs usually have weird phrasing or words that do not make sense together.

3. Use Detection Tools

Some tools scan audio files to find AI music. They check the sound waves for patterns humans cannot hear. You can use the [Quicksilver Browser Extension](https://news.uchicago.edu/story/song-ai-generated-uchicago-scientists-create-browser-extension-check) to scan for AI audio. Platforms like [SubmitHub](https://www.submithub.com/story/ai-song-checker) offer specialized checks for songs. You can also try the [Modulate AI Music Detection API](https://www.modulate.ai/api/ai-music-detection) for detailed audio analysis.
