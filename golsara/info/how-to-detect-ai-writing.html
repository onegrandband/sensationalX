How to Spot AI Writing

You can detect AI writing by looking for perfectly even sentence lengths, a lack of deep personal emotion, and repetitive "buzzwords". You can also use free detection tools to scan the text, though it is best to check using more than one method.

Manual Signs of AI Writing

Before using tools, you can spot AI-generated content by checking the writing's style:

  • Even-handed rhythm: Human writing has sentences of mixed lengths. AI often writes sentences that are all the exact same length.
  • Overuse of buzzwords: AI frequently uses words like delve, tapestry, or treasure trove.
  • Lack of depth: AI avoids specific personal stories and often lacks deep analysis.
  • Factual errors: AI may confidently state facts or quotes that are completely made up.

Why Detectors Can Fail

AI detectors are not $100\%$ accurate. Sometimes, they mistakenly flag human writing as AI. This happens often to people writing in a second language, as their simpler sentences match AI patterns. For this reason, you should always combine manual checks with tool scans.
