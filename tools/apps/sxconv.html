Welcome to SX Converter

SXConv is a lightweight, no-nonsense utility for converting media, archives, and documents. Built from the ground up in C++, it prioritizes speed, low memory usage, and maximum compatibility over flashy interfaces.

Whether you are running a brand new workstation or a dusty Pentium III in your basement, SXConv gets the job done without freezing your system.

Why use SXConv?

  • Incredibly Small Footprint: The entire program is under 2 megabytes.
  • Batch Processing: Convert thousands of files overnight with a single click.
  • Command-Line Ready: Full CLI support for advanced users and batch scripters.
  • Portable: Run it directly from a USB flash drive. No registry keys required!
  • Totally Free: SXConv is open-source (GPLv2). No trials, no watermarks, no bundled toolbars.

System Requirements

SXConv is highly optimized. It requires a minimum of a 486 processor, 16MB of RAM, and 5MB of free hard disk space. For processing large video files, a Pentium III or newer is highly recommended.
