The Flags of our Community

Each stripe holds a story, a history, and a promise of visibility.

Gilbert Baker Pride Flag

Originally designed in 1978, representing life, healing, sunlight, nature, harmony, and spirit.

Transgender Flag

Designed by Monica Helms in 1999. Light blue and pink represent traditional baby colors, while white represents transitioning or neutral gender identity.

Non-Binary Flag

Created by Kye Rowan in 2014. Yellow represents gender outside the binary, white for multi-gender, purple for a mix, and black for agender.
